# Sources And Signals

A discovery pool, not an endorsement, complete reading list or daily quota.
Follow current material to its original evidence. The additions below were checked
against public source pages on November 21, 2026; recheck dates and claims at use.
Access to a landing page is not evidence that every linked article was read.

## Product, Engineering And Open Ecosystems

- OpenAI official news, engineering, documentation, release notes and pricing;
  Anthropic [engineering](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering), Claude release
  notes and Claude Code project materials; Google AI/Gemini developer releases.
- [GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/), Copilot updates and original
  project repositories/releases. Cloud/platform changes from AWS, Azure,
  Cloudflare, Vercel, Supabase and relevant database/search providers.
- Hugging Face model and dataset pages, original papers and
  [model cards](https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/model-cards). Check licenses and
  restrictions rather than calling every downloadable model open source.
- Relevant release notes: LangChain, LlamaIndex, DSPy, vLLM, llama.cpp, Ollama,
  OpenTelemetry, PostgreSQL, DuckDB, ClickHouse, Qdrant, Weaviate and Milvus.
  Select based on current user workflows, not a requirement to scan every project.
- [GitHub Trending](https://github.com/trending) is standing discovery for emerging
  projects. Inspect activity, issues, actual releases and maintainability; trending
  alone is not sustained traction, independent validation or commercial adoption.
- [AAIF](https://aaif.io/), Linux Foundation, Apache, Eclipse, Mozilla, MLCommons,
  AI Alliance and relevant W3C/IETF work: source projects, interoperability,
  standards status and implementation evidence, not just membership announcements.

## Evaluation, Security And Accessibility

- [METR research](https://metr.org/research/): task performance, evaluation methods
  and productivity/capability evidence. Distinguish published research from notes,
  self-reports and tentative results.
- [Epoch AI](https://epoch.ai/): data, capability measurements, economics,
  infrastructure and adoption. Inspect methods and uncertainty, not only charts.
- [Stanford AI Index](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index): periodic contextual data
  on adoption, investment and social impacts; do not repackage annual statistics
  as a new daily event or assume institutional branding makes every claim settled.
- [NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework): original
  risk guidance and related publications; distinguish voluntary guidance, draft
  standards, final standards and binding rules.
- [OWASP GenAI Security Project](https://genai.owasp.org/): agent/application
  security guidance and incident analysis. Follow exploit and mitigation claims to
  primary disclosures; avoid amplifying unverified vulnerability assertions.
- [W3C WAI](https://www.w3.org/WAI/): accessibility standards and implementation
  guidance. Changes matter when they affect real user access or product decisions,
  not merely as another compliance headline.

## Economic And Business Signals

Look for labor and productivity research, enterprise spending/adoption, total
cost per successful workflow, infrastructure/energy/capex, pricing and margin
changes. Prefer original studies, official statistics, audited filings/earnings
materials and original cost disclosures. Provider economic studies are useful
but represent their own population and methods, not the entire labor market.

Distinguish an AI-native challenger's claimed lower labor/token costs from full
delivery economics: acquisition, integration, review, failures, support and risk.
Record the incumbent job/service being challenged, proposed wedge, buyer and
cheap validation; an interesting category is not a business opportunity proved.

## Events And Public-Interest Communities

- [MozFest](https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/festival/): current editions,
  sessions, recordings, public-interest themes and practitioner outcomes.
- AGNTCon + MCPCon and related [AAIF events](https://aaif.io/): standards work,
  sessions, implementations and launch signals. Verify the edition from the
  organizer; do not keep reporting a concluded conference as upcoming.
- AI Engineer, NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, KDD, ACL/EMNLP, COLM, MLSys, ODSC,
  Applied AI Conference, VB Transform, HumanX, TED AI, SXSW AI tracks, SaaStr,
  Cerebral Valley/Newcomer and relevant local/provider/community events.
  These are candidate venues, not verified current deadlines or user travel plans.
- Prefer official program, CFP and registration pages. Luma/Eventbrite/Meetup can
  help locate an organizer, but do not independently establish final deadlines.

Choose event angles from the user's actual experience and interests. Never
assume an employer, personal affiliation, speaking credentials or preferred city.
Past event outcomes can matter even when no attendance action remains.
